Welcome to Roatan Island Style Tours

Founded in 2019 by co-founders Kerwin Borden and Charleston Jackson, Roatan Island Style Tours was built on a simple promise: to share the raw beauty, vibrant turquoise waters, and warm hospitality of our island home with visitors from around the globe.

Like many local businesses, our initial momentum paused during the global pandemic. But our passion for host hospitality and island life never wavered. Today, we are back stronger than ever, giving our guests exclusive, customized access to Roatan's finest beaches, hidden bays, and private coastal tours.

As a locally owned outfit, our main priority is to deliver far more than a standard excursion. We bring you personalized customer service, unmatched local knowledge, and an incredible adventure where you can truly relax and have a blast in genuine island style.
